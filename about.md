### Faris Chebib

Senior Engineer at [GeneDx](https://genedx.com) ($WGS), working at the intersection of
genomics, AI, and the software that makes sense of both.

Background in cognitive science, 20 years building for the web, and a Private Pilot
Certificate — the kind of credential that signals checklist discipline and systems thinking
in three dimensions.

I run the [Salt Lake City Python meetup](https://slcpy.com) (co-founded 2013),
teach data and web development at [hacktivities.org](https://hacktivities.org),
and write about the technical side of things at [faris.ai](https://faris.ai).

---

### The through-line

Computation in service of understanding living systems — genes, neurons, and the systems
that connect them.
